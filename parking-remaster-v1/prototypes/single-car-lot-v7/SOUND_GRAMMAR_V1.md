# Temporary Sound Grammar V1

V7 keeps motion timing from V6 and adds temporary synthesized audio only to judge rhythm.

- CHARGE: low engine rev rises; rear-tire noise builds while stationary.
- FIRST BLAST: short launch chirp plus nonlinear engine pitch rise.
- SNAP: engine briefly dips while tire squeal peaks.
- RELOAD: second short rev-hold, audibly separated from the first blast.
- EXIT: strongest pitch rise and a short second chirp.

Hard rule: never use one flat engine loop across the full maneuver.
Final recorded/vehicle-specific SFX remain downstream of core motion/puzzle approval.
